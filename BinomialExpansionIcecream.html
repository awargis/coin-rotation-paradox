📜 Did You Know?

Around 200 BCE, Indian scholar Pingala developed methods for combinatorics in his work on Sanskrit poetry meters. His "Meru Prastāra" is the earliest known version of what is now called Pascal's Triangle, a key element in binomial expansions, which are a special case of the multinomial theorem.
