Necklace Symmetry Demonstration

How clockwise and anti-clockwise arrangements are identical

Current direction: Clockwise ↻

In circular arrangements like necklaces, clockwise and anti-clockwise orderings are considered identical because you can simply flip the necklace over to transform one into the other.

This demonstrates the mathematical concept of rotational symmetry where two arrangements are considered the same if one can be rotated or reflected to obtain the other.
