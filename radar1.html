HOW IT WORKS

TIME DIFFERENCE OF ARRIVAL

Radar stations emit signals that reflect off targets . The reflected signals arrive at different stations at slightly different times.

This time difference creates a constant difference in distance between the target and each pair of stations.

THE HYPERBOLA PRINCIPLE

A hyperbola is defined as the set of points where the difference of distances to two fixed points (foci) is constant.

In radar systems:
- Foci = Radar stations
- Constant difference = c × Δt (speed of light × time difference)

TARGET RESOLUTION

Each pair of radar stations defines a hyperbola where the target must lie. With multiple pairs:

1. Two stations → Target lies on a hyperbola curve
2. Three stations → Intersection of two hyperbolas gives exact location
3. Four stations → Precision 3D location tracking

APPLICATIONS
