🎯 The Hyperbola Adventure

Welcome to Hyperbola Explorer! You're about to discover one of the most fascinating curves in mathematics.

Mission: You're a radar operator tracking a mysterious signal source. Your two receivers (R1 and R2) detect signals, but there's a time difference!

Key Insight: The set of all points where the difference in distances to two fixed points (foci) is constant forms a hyperbola!

Drag the green signal source and watch the magic unfold!
