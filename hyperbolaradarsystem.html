🚀 Advanced Space Navigation Mission

Welcome to Space Explorer: Advanced Astronaut Tracking! You're an astronaut floating in space, trying to locate the International Space Station using hyperbolic navigation.

Mission: You've deployed two navigation beacons (B1 and B2) that detect signals from the space station with time differences!

Key Insight: The set of all points where the difference in distances to two fixed points (beacons) is constant forms a hyperbola!

New Features: Mission objectives, interactive tutorial, performance metrics, sound effects, and enhanced visuals!
