Fermat’s Principle — Mirror Reflection (Least Time)

Drag points A and B or adjust the mirror height to see how light chooses the reflection point that minimizes travel time.
Fermat’s Principle (Reflection): Light reflects such that the total path is shortest, where the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection.
Roshni sidhe-run mein nahi jaati, woh mirror par aisi jagah se takraati hai ke use sabse kam samay lage. Yahi hai Fermat ka principle reflection ke liye!
