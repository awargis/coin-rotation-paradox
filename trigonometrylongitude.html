📐 Problem: Arc Distance via Latitude

Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and Miami, Florida, lie approximately on the same meridian. Pittsburgh has a latitude of 40.5°N, and Miami has a latitude of 25.5°N. Find the distance between these two cities. (Earth radius = 3960 mi)
📍 Pittsburgh: 40.5°N 📍 Miami: 25.5°N 🌍 R = 3960 mi
📏 Arc Distance (s) = 1037 miles
\( s = R \cdot \theta_{\text{rad}} \)
