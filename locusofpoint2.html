Move the courier while maintaining equal distance from the fountain and bench. Watch as the mathematical equation builds in real-time with stunning visual effects.

Key Concept: The set of points equidistant from two fixed points forms a perpendicular bisector (in 2D) or a plane (in 3D). This is visualized as a golden path with cinematic effects.
