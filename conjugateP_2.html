Property 2: Constant Sum Property

For any ellipse, the sum of the squares of two conjugate semi-diameters is always constant:

CP² + CD² = a² + b²

Where CP and CD are conjugate semi-diameters.

Algebraic Proof

CP² = a²cos²φ + b²sin²φ
CD² = a²sin²φ + b²cos²φ
CP² + CD² = a²(cos²φ + sin²φ) + b²(sin²φ + cos²φ)
CP² + CD² = a² + b²
