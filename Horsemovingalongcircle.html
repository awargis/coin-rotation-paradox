Horse Shadow Physics

Visualizing circular motion and light projection in real-time

Physics Problem Statement

A horse runs along a circle with a speed of 20 km/hr. A lantern is at the center of the circle. A fence is along the tangent to the circle at the point at which the horse starts.

The speed with which the shadow of the horse moves along the fence at the moment when it covers 1/8 of the circle (in km/hr) is?

This problem demonstrates the relationship between angular motion and linear projection. As the horse moves along the circular path, its shadow moves along the tangent fence at a varying speed that depends on the horse's angular position.

Light travels in straight lines from the lantern
Horse moves at constant tangential speed
Shadow speed = v / cos(θ)
At 45°: 20 / cos(45°) = 20√2 ≈ 28.28 km/hr
