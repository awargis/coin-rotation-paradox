Parabola: y² = 4ax

Parabola Properties

The parabola is defined by the equation:

y² = 4(1.0)x = 4.0x

• Focus: F(1.0, 0)

• Directrix: x = -1.0

• Vertex: (0, 0)

Normals & Conormal Points

From point P(h,k), normals can be drawn to the parabola.

The cubic equation for the slopes (t):

t³ + (2.0 - 3.0)t - 4.0 = 0

Roots t₁, t₂, t₃ give the conormal points A, B, C.

Geometric Properties

• The circle through A, B, C also passes through the vertex (0,0).

• The centroid of triangle ABC always lies on the X-axis.

• P(h,k) must be outside the red **Evolute** curve for 3 real normals to exist.
