🧠 Key Concepts for JEE

This simulation demonstrates the core principle of Relative Velocity.

  • Frame of Reference: Physics problems can be analyzed from different viewpoints. The 'Ground Frame' is a stationary observer's view. The 'Person's Frame' is what the moving person experiences.
  • Vector Addition/Subtraction: The velocity of rain as seen by the person ($\mathbf{v}_{r,p}$) is the velocity of rain relative to the ground ($\mathbf{v}_{r,g}$) minus the velocity of the person relative to the ground ($\mathbf{v}_{p,g}$).
    $$ \mathbf{v}_{r,p} = \mathbf{v}_{r,g} - \mathbf{v}_{p,g} $$
  • Effect of Wind: Wind adds a horizontal component to the rain's velocity relative to the ground. The total velocity of rain w.r.t ground becomes:
    $$ \mathbf{v}_{r,g} = \mathbf{v}_{\text{rain}} + \mathbf{v}_{\text{wind}} $$

🎯 Challenge Questions

  • Set the wind speed to be equal and opposite to the person's speed. What do you observe about the angle of the rain in the person's frame? Why?
  • If the person wants the rain to appear to fall perfectly vertically, what should be the relationship between their speed and the wind speed?
  • How does increasing the rain's vertical speed ($v_r$) affect the angle ($\theta$) you need to hold the umbrella at, for a fixed person and wind speed? Does it increase or decrease?
