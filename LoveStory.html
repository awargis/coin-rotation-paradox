Mathematical Foundation

The system is described by:

dR/dt = a·P

dP/dt = -b·R - c·P

Where:

  • R: Rahul's affection level
  • P: Priya's affection level
  • a: Rahul's sensitivity to Priya's affection
  • b: Priya's resistance to Rahul's affection
  • c: Damping coefficient (dissipation of emotion)

This is a linear system of differential equations. The eigenvalues of the coefficient matrix determine the behavior:

λ = [-c ± √(c² - 4ab)] / 2

When c=0, the system oscillates with frequency √(ab). With damping, the system spirals toward equilibrium at (0,0).
