Angle Between Two Planes

📚 Concept Explanation

The angle between two planes is defined as the angle between their normal vectors. This visualization demonstrates how to calculate this angle in 3D space.

🧮 Mathematical Formula
cos(θ) = (n₁ · n₂) / (|n₁| |n₂|)
θ = 0.00°
1. Find normal vectors n₁ and n₂ for each plane
2. Calculate the dot product: n₁ · n₂
3. Calculate magnitudes: |n₁| and |n₂|
4. Apply formula: θ = acos((n₁ · n₂) / (|n₁| |n₂|))
