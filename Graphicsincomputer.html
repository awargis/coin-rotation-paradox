About This Visualization

This demonstration shows how complex multiplication rotates points in the 2D plane. Each point z = x + iy is multiplied by eiθ = cosθ + i sinθ, resulting in a rotation by angle θ.

Hover over any point to see its complex coordinates. Use the controls to adjust the rotation and explore different visualization modes.
