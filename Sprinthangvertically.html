Spring-Mass System Animation

An object fastened to the end of a vertical spring is stretched 4 cm beyond its rest position and released at time t=0. Its position at time t is given by s(t) = 4 cos(t). This animation visualizes the motion and the relationship between position, velocity, and acceleration.

Physics of the Spring-Mass System

Position: s(t) = 4 cos(t)    Velocity: v(t) = ds/dt = -4 sin(t)    Acceleration: a(t) = dv/dt = -4 cos(t)

This animation demonstrates simple harmonic motion, where the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement from equilibrium (Hooke's Law).

Key Observations

  • At t=0, position is maximum (4 cm)
  • Velocity is zero when displacement is maximum
  • Acceleration is maximum when displacement is maximum
  • Velocity is maximum when passing through equilibrium

Phase Relationships

  • Position and acceleration are in phase (differ by 0°)
  • Velocity leads position by 90° (π/2 radians)
  • Acceleration leads velocity by 90° (π/2 radians)

Energy Conservation

  • Potential energy is maximum at extremes
  • Kinetic energy is maximum at equilibrium
  • Total mechanical energy remains constant
