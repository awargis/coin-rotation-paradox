🚀 Space Navigation Mission

Welcome to Space Explorer! You're an astronaut floating in space, trying to locate the International Space Station.

Mission: You've deployed two navigation beacons (B1 and B2) that detect signals from the space station with time differences!

Key Insight: The set of all points where the difference in distances to two fixed points (beacons) is constant forms a hyperbola!

Drag the astronaut and watch as the hyperbolic navigation paths reveal the possible locations of the space station!
