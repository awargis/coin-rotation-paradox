Physics Problem

A mass on a spring vibrates horizontally on a smooth level surface. Its equation of motion is:

\( x(t) = 8 \sin t \)

where \( t \) is in seconds and \( x \) in centimeters.

Solution

(a) Velocity and Acceleration at time \( t \):

Velocity is the derivative of position with respect to time:

\( v(t) = \frac{dx}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt}(8 \sin t) = 8 \cos t \) cm/s

Acceleration is the derivative of velocity with respect to time:

\( a(t) = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt}(8 \cos t) = -8 \sin t \) cm/s²
(b) Position, Velocity, and Acceleration at \( t = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \):

Position at \( t = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \):

\( x\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 8 \sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 8 \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 4\sqrt{3} \approx 6.93 \) cm

Velocity at \( t = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \):

\( v\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 8 \cos\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 8 \times \left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right) = -4 \) cm/s

Acceleration at \( t = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \):

\( a\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right) = -8 \sin\left(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\right) = -8 \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = -4\sqrt{3} \approx -6.93 \) cm/s²

Direction: ← Moving Left

(Since velocity is negative at \( t = \dfrac{2\pi}{3} \))
