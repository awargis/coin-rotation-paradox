Mathematical Concept - Conormal Points

−

For an ellipse with equation x²/a² + y²/b² = 1, the conormal points are the points where the normals from a given external point intersect the ellipse.

Key Mathematical Facts:

  • Four normals can be drawn from an external point to a central conic
  • The four feet of normals are called conormal points
  • These points lie on a rectangular hyperbola called the Apollonian rectangular hyperbola
  • The sum of eccentric angles of conormal points is an odd multiple of π

Equation of Normal at point (a cosθ, b sinθ):
