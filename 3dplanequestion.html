Concept Breakdown

The cube visualized has a side length $L=5$. Its vertices are defined in a coordinate system where the origin is not $(0, 0, 0)$, but the cube is offset (translated) in space. All 8 vertices are uniquely defined by combining the starting point $(1, 0, -1)$ with the three dimension vectors:

  • X-axis change: $1 \rightarrow -4$ (a displacement of $-5$)
  • Y-axis change: $0 \rightarrow -5$ (a displacement of $-5$)
  • Z-axis change: $-1 \rightarrow 4$ (a displacement of $+5$)

Understanding these displacements is key for vector and scalar triple product problems. The vectors forming the edges of the cube are $\vec{a} = (-5, 0, 0)$, $\vec{b} = (0, -5, 0)$, and $\vec{c} = (0, 0, 5)$.
