Understanding Locus

In coordinate geometry, a locus is a set of points that satisfy a given condition or rule.

As the laser moves, it traces the path of all points that follow the mathematical rule shown below.

y = x²

This visualization shows how the locus forms over time, creating a visible geometric shape from individual points.
