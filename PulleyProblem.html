Related Rates: Pulley System

Cinematic Physics Visualization

Problem Statement

Two carts, A and B, are connected by a rope 39 ft long passing over a pulley located 12 ft above point Q. Cart A is moving away from Q at a constant speed of 2 ft/s. How fast is cart B moving toward Q when cart A is 5 ft from Q?

Mathematical Solution

Step 1: Geometric constraint
\[ \sqrt{a^2 + 12^2} + \sqrt{b^2 + 12^2} = 39 \]

Where \(a\) is distance from A to Q, \(b\) is distance from B to Q.

Step 2: Solve for \(b\) when \(a = 5\)
\[ \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} + \sqrt{b^2 + 12^2} = 39 \\ 13 + \sqrt{b^2 + 144} = 39 \\ \sqrt{b^2 + 144} = 26 \\ b^2 = 532 \\ b = \sqrt{532} \approx 23.07 \text{ ft} \]
Step 3: Differentiate with respect to time
\[ \frac{a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 144}} \frac{da}{dt} + \frac{b}{\sqrt{b^2 + 144}} \frac{db}{dt} = 0 \]
Step 4: Solve for \(\frac{db}{dt}\)
\[ \frac{5}{13} \cdot 2 + \frac{23.07}{26} \frac{db}{dt} = 0 \\ \frac{db}{dt} = -\frac{10}{23.07} \approx -0.867 \text{ ft/s} \]
Final Answer
≈ 0.867 ft/s toward Q
