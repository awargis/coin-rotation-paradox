ELLIPSE REFLECTION THEOREM

The reflection of focus S₁ in any tangent lies on the line joining point P to focus S₂

CONTROLS

MATHEMATICAL PROOF

Ellipse Equation: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Foci: S₁(-c,0), S₂(c,0) where c² = a² - b²

Tangent at P(acosθ, bsinθ):
(x cosθ)/a + (y sinθ)/b = 1

Reflection Property:
S₁' (reflection of S₁) always lies on PS₂

COLLINEARITY: PERFECT
Angle deviation: 0.00°
