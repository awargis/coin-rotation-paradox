Ellipse Reflection Theorem

The reflection of focus S₁ in any tangent lies on line PS₂

Mathematical Proof

For ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1

Foci: S₁(-c,0), S₂(c,0) where c² = a² - b²

Tangent at P(acosθ, bsinθ):
(x cosθ)/a + (y sinθ)/b = 1

Reflection S₁' lies on PS₂ by ellipse property
