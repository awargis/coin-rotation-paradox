Spotlight Related Rates Animation

A visualization of how the angle θ changes as a man walks away from a spotlight. The spotlight constantly focuses on the man as he moves horizontally at 4 ft/s.

Physics Panel

Physics & Calculus

Position (x): 15 ft
Angle (θ): 0.6435 rad
dθ/dt: 0.0303 rad/s
Time: 0.0 s
Related Rates Formula:
dθ/dt = (20 × dx/dt) / (20² + x²)

As the man walks away from the spotlight:

  • The angle θ decreases at a rate proportional to the man's speed
  • The rate of change (dθ/dt) decreases as the man moves farther away
  • When the man is close to the spotlight, the angle changes rapidly
  • When the man is far away, the angle changes very slowly
