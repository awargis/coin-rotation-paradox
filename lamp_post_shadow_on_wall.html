A man walks at 4 ft/s • The spotlight rotates to follow him

📐 MATHEMATICAL MODEL ▼ Derivation
\[ \tan\theta = \frac{x}{20} \]
\[ \frac{d}{dt}[\tan\theta] = \frac{d}{dt}\left[\frac{x}{20}\right] \]
\[ \sec^2\theta \cdot \frac{d\theta}{dt} = \frac{1}{20}\frac{dx}{dt} \]
Given: \( \frac{dx}{dt} = 4\ \text{ft/s} \) , at \( x = 15\ \text{ft} \)
\[ \tan\theta = \frac{15}{20}=0.75,\quad \sec^2\theta = 1+0.75^2 = 1.5625 \]
\[ \frac{d\theta}{dt} = \frac{4}{20 \times 1.5625} = 0.128\ \text{rad/s} \]
⭐ Angular velocity = 0.128 rad/s
