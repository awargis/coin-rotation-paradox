Q1. Unmarked Squads Division

Parade ground par bina naam ki (unmarked) squads banani hain. Kitne tareeqe honge agar:

  • (a) Squad sizes (3, 2, 1) hon vs 4 men mein (3, 1) hon?
  • (b) 6 men ki (2, 2, 2) barabar squads hon, aur agar M1 & M2 ek saath na rehna chahein?
  • (c) 5 men ki (2, 2, 1) hybrid squads hon?
(a) 6!/(3!2!1!) = 60 | 4!/(3!1!) = 4
(b) All: 5×3×1 = 15 | M1≠M2: 4×3×1 = 12
(c) 5!/(2!2!1! × 2!) = 15 ways
