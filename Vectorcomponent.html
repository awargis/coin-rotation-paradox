3D Vector Components

In 3D space, any vector can be expressed in terms of its components along the x, y, and z axes.

v = xi + yj + zk

Where:

  • x is the component along the x-axis (i direction)
  • y is the component along the y-axis (j direction)
  • z is the component along the z-axis (k direction)

The perpendicular from the point to the XY-plane helps visualize the z-component.

Right-Handed System: Point your right hand's index finger along X (blue), middle finger along Y (red), and thumb will point along Z (green).
