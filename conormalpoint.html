Parabola: y² = 4x

Parabola Properties

The parabola is defined by the equation:

y² = 4ax (with a = 1)

• Focus: F(1, 0)

• Directrix: x = -1

• Vertex: (0, 0)

Normals & Conormal Points

From point P(h,k), three normals can be drawn to the parabola.

The cubic equation for the slopes:

t³ + (2a - h)t - k = 0

Roots t₁, t₂, t₃ give the conormal points A, B, C.
