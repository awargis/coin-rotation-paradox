In a school playground, 5 boys and 5 girls are asked to stand in a queue...

Let n be the number of ways in which 5 boys and 5 girls can stand in a queue such that all the girls stand consecutively. Let m be the number of ways in which 5 boys and 5 girls can stand in a queue such that exactly four girls stand consecutively. Then the value of m/n is ______

Calculating n

Step 1: Treat 5 girls as 1 block

Step 2: Arrange these 6 units = 6!

Calculating m

Step 1: Choose 4 girls to form block = C(5,4)

Step 2: Arrange block + 5 boys + 1 girl = 7 units → 7!

Step 3: Arrange girls inside block = 4!

Step 4: Arrange boys = 5!

Total m = C(5,4) × 7! × 4! × 5!

n = 6! × 5!

m = C(5,4) × 7! × 4! × 5!

m/n = [C(5,4) × 7! × 4! × 5!] / [6! × 5!]
= [5 × 7! × 4!] / [6 × 5! × 5!]
= [5 × 7 × 6 × 5! × 4!] / [6 × 5!]
= 5 × 7 × 4!
= 35 × 24
= 840
