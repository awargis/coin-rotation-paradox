Precise Definition

Let \( f \) be a function defined on some interval \((a, \infty)\). Then \[ \lim_{x \to \infty} f(x) = L \] means that for every \(\varepsilon > 0\) there is a corresponding number \( N \) such that \[ \text{if} \quad x > N \quad \text{then} \quad |f(x) - L| < \varepsilon \]
