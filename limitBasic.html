🥪 Sandwich Theorem

Evaluate $\lim_{x \to 0} x^2 \sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$. The function is trapped between $x^2$ and $-x^2$.

$g(x) = x^2$ (Upper Bound)
$f(x) = x^2 \sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$
$h(x) = -x^2$ (Lower Bound)
Since $h(x) \le f(x) \le g(x)$
and $\lim_{x \to 0} h(x) = \lim_{x \to 0} g(x) = 0$,
$$\therefore \lim_{x \to 0} f(x) = 0$$
