Understanding Weighted Mean

The combined mean (weighted mean) is calculated as (n₁·m₁ + n₂·m₂) / (n₁ + n₂). Notice how the larger group (Group B with 15 points) pulls the combined mean toward its own mean value. Drag individual points to see how the means update in real time.
