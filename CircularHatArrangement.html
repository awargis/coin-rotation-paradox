Circular Hat Arrangement Problem

Five people seated in a circle with hats of three colors, no two adjacent with same color

🎩
🎩

Mathematical Solution

Number of people (n) = 5
Hat colors (q) = 3
Condition: Adjacent persons ≠ same color
Formula: (q-1)^n + (q-1)(-1)^n
Substitution: (3-1)^5 + (3-1)(-1)^5
Calculation: 32 - 2 = 30 ways
