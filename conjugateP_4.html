Ellipse Property 4: Conjugate Diameters & Tangent Parallelogram

Visualizing how tangents at the endpoints of conjugate diameters form a parallelogram with constant area

Property Statement

The tangents at the endpoints of conjugate diameters form a parallelogram whose area equals the product of the semi-axes (a × b).

Understanding the Property

Conjugate Diameters

Two diameters of an ellipse are conjugate if each diameter is parallel to the tangent at the endpoint of the other diameter.

Tangent Parallelogram

When tangents are drawn at the endpoints of conjugate diameters, they intersect to form a parallelogram.

Constant Area

The area of this parallelogram remains constant regardless of which pair of conjugate diameters is chosen, and equals a × b, where a and b are the semi-major and semi-minor axes.
