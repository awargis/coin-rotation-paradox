Factory Visit Planner

An engineer has to visit a factory exactly 4 times in the first 15 days of June 2021, but no two visits can be on consecutive days. Find the number of possible ways.

June 2021 - First 15 Days

Mathematical Solution

We need to choose 4 days out of 15 with no two consecutive.

Let the chosen days be: d₁, d₂, d₃, d₄

We can transform the problem by defining:

x₁ = d₁, x₂ = d₂ - d₁ - 1, ..., x₅ = 15 - d₄

Where x₁ ≥ 1, x₅ ≥ 0, and x₂, x₃, x₄ ≥ 1

Then the equation becomes: x₁ + x₂ + x₃ + x₄ + x₅ = 15 - 4 + 1 = 12

Which is equivalent to: C(12, 4) = 495

Valid combinations: 0 / 495
