Alternating Arrangements

A Visual Explanation of Combinatorial Patterns

We have 5 girls and 5 boys that need to be arranged in a row such that boys and girls alternate.

How many different arrangements are possible?

Understanding the Problem

When arranging 5 boys and 5 girls alternately in a row, we have two possible patterns:

1. Pattern starting with a boy: B-G-B-G-B-G-B-G-B-G

2. Pattern starting with a girl: G-B-G-B-G-B-G-B-G-B

For each pattern, the 5 boys can be arranged in 5! = 120 ways, and the 5 girls can be arranged in 5! = 120 ways.

Since these are independent arrangements, we multiply these values for each pattern.

Total arrangements = 2 × (5! × 5!) = 2 × (120 × 120)
28,800 Possible Arrangements
