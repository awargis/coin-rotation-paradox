Rolle's Theorem

Let \( f \) be a function that satisfies the following three conditions:

  • Continuity: \( f \) is continuous on the closed interval \([a, b]\)
  • Differentiability: \( f \) is differentiable on the open interval \((a, b)\)
  • Equal Endpoints: \( f(a) = f(b) \)

Conclusion: Then there exists at least one number \( c \) in \((a, b)\) such that:

\( f'(c) = 0 \)

This implies the existence of at least one point where the tangent line is horizontal.
