CONJUGATE DIAMETERS

"Two diameters are conjugate if their parametric angles differ by 90°."
(Equivalently: Each bisects chords parallel to the other)
ECCENTRIC ANGLES
Point P (φ): 45°
Point D (φ + 90°): 135°
Difference: 90°
Parametric Equations:
P: (a·cos φ, b·sin φ)
D: (a·cos(φ+90°), b·sin(φ+90°))
ALGEBRAIC PROOF
1. Slope of CP (Point P at φ): m₁ = (b/a) tan(φ)
2. Slope of CD (Point D at φ + 90°): m₂ = (b/a) tan(φ + 90°)
3. Trig Identity: tan(φ + 90°) = -cot(φ) m₂ = (b/a) [-1/tan(φ)]
4. Product of Slopes: m₁ × m₂ = - (b²/a²)
