Triangle Centers

Centroid (G)

The point where the three medians of the triangle intersect. It always lies inside the triangle.

G = ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3)

Properties: Center of mass, divides medians in 2:1 ratio.

Incenter (I)

The point where the internal angle bisectors of the triangle intersect. Center of the inscribed circle.

I = ( (ax₁+bx₂+cx₃)/(a+b+c), (ay₁+by₂+cy₃)/(a+b+c) )

Properties: Equidistant from all sides, center of incircle.

Circumcenter (O)

The point where the perpendicular bisectors of the sides intersect. Center of the circumscribed circle.

Calculated by intersection of perpendicular bisectors

Properties: Equidistant from all vertices, may lie outside the triangle.

Orthocenter (H)

The point where the three altitudes of the triangle intersect.

Calculated by intersection of altitudes

Properties: In acute triangles, it lies inside. In obtuse triangles, it lies outside.
