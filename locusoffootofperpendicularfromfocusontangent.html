Mathematical Proof:

For ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1, tangent at (x₁,y₁) is: xx₁/a² + yy₁/b² = 1

Perpendicular from focus (c,0) to tangent has foot (h,k) satisfying:

h = a²x₁/(a² + b²), k = -b²y₁/(a² + b²)

Thus h² + k² = a⁴x₁²/(a²+b²)² + b⁴y₁²/(a²+b²)²

Since x₁²/a² + y₁²/b² = 1, we get h² + k² = a²

Hence, foot lies on auxiliary circle: x² + y² = a²
