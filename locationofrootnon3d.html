Root Location Conditions

Both roots less than k

For both roots of \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) to be less than k:

1. Discriminant \(D = b^2 - 4ac \geq 0\)

2. \(af(k) > 0\)

3. \(k > -\frac{b}{2a}\)

Both roots greater than k

For both roots of \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) to be greater than k:

1. Discriminant \(D = b^2 - 4ac \geq 0\)

2. \(af(k) > 0\)

3. \(k < -\frac{b}{2a}\)

Roots between k₁ and k₂ (k₁ < k₂)

For both roots of \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) to lie between k₁ and k₂:

1. Discriminant \(D = b^2 - 4ac \geq 0\)

2. \(af(k₁) > 0\)

3. \(af(k₂) > 0\)

4. \(k₁ < -\frac{b}{2a} < k₂\)

Example: Both roots less than 1

Find values of \(m\) where both roots of \(x^2 - mx + 1 = 0\) are less than 1.

Solution:

Let \(f(x) = x^2 - mx + 1\). Conditions for both roots < 1:

1. Discriminant \(D \geq 0\):

\[D = (-m)^2 - 4(1)(1) = m^2 - 4 \geq 0\]

\(\Rightarrow m \in (-\infty, -2] \cup [2, \infty)\)

2. \(a \cdot f(1) > 0\):

\[f(1) = 1 - m + 1 = 2 - m\]

\[1 \cdot (2 - m) > 0 \Rightarrow m < 2\]

3. \(k > -\frac{b}{2a}\) (with k=1):

\[1 > -\frac{-m}{2(1)} \Rightarrow 1 > \frac{m}{2} \Rightarrow m < 2\]

Intersection of all conditions: \(m \in (-\infty, -2]\)
